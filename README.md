## 📌 Executive Overview

A food discovery and online restaurant ordering web application. Features categorized meal menus (Pizzas, Burgers, Asian, Desserts), meal item previews, real-time cart calculations, and delivery checkout workflows.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Capabilities

- **Menu Categorization:** Filter dishes by cuisine type, dietary preferences, or top-rated meals.
- **Dynamic Food Cart:** Add meals with custom quantity increments, price recalculation, and order review modal.
- **High-Appeal Food Cards:** Appetizing meal cards showcasing nutritional badges, prices, and high-res imagery.
- **Mobile-Optimized:** Quick food ordering experience on mobile browsers.
